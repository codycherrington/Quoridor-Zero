Quoridor Zero

Moves

    How to play

    Race your pawn to the far side. Each turn either move one square (click a dotted square) or place a wall (hover the grooves between squares and click; on touch screens tap once to preview, again to place).

    Walls are two squares long, can't overlap or cross, and may never completely cut off a player's path. Facing pawns can be jumped; if a wall is behind, jump diagonally.

    Notation: squares a1–i9; walls are named by the square to their lower-left plus h/v, e.g. e3h.
